# Lesson Plan 16.1: Software Economics, Agile Delivery & Capstone (`Std 19.0`)

**Unit:** Unit 16 (`30 hrs` Year 2 Sem 4 `CTE/CIS 202` SaaS Sprint + Agile Prep · or 1-Sem Week 18 Days 86–90) | **Duration:** 5 Class Periods × 50 min | **Stds:** `19.1`–`19.8` + Capstone

## Bloom's Objective Ladder
- **L1–L2:** Contrast Waterfall, Top-Down, and Agile/Scrum (`19.1`); list store deployment rules (`19.2`), monetization strategies (`19.5`), and unit economics metrics (`19.4, 19.7`).
- **L3 Apply:** Calculate net per-subscriber contribution, monthly break-even count, and `LTV:CAC` ratios cleanly in Node.
- **L4 Analyze:** Spot unprofitable `LTV < 3*CAC` churn leaks & scope-creep schedule overruns (`19.6–19.7`).
- **L5–L6 Evaluate / Create:** Cut MVP backlog decisively and pitch a tested full-semester **JavaScript Capstone**.

## Pacing & Teacher Answer Keys
- **Day 1:** Pre-Lesson 16.1 (4m), Slides + Agile vs Waterfall + App Store Case Study (25m), Part A & `appLaunchPlanner.js`.
- **Day 2–4:** **Full-Semester Capstone Sprint (`unit-16-semester-capstone`)** combining Auth/Security (`1.0`), Storage/CRUD (`16.0`), Async/Tests (`12.0, 18.0`), and Business Plan (`19.0`).
- **Day 5:** Capstone Demo Day + Peer Code Review + Post-Lesson 16.1 Exit Ticket & Post-Unit Summative Evaluation.
- **Pre 16.1 Key:** 1) Waterfall tests at end; Agile ships working increments every sprint for live feedback; 2) Minimum Viable Product—launches core problem solver fast to validate demand before wasting months on unused features; 3) `$8.00`; 4) Every customer loses `-$20`, accelerating bankruptcy as marketing scales!
- **Post 16.1 Key:** 1) `SaaS Subscription`, `Freemium`, `Ad-Supported`, `One-Time License`, `Usage-Based`; 2) Top-Down decomposes technical modules cleanly; Retrospectives improve team velocity/process after each sprint; 3) LTV=`$80`, Max CAC=`$26.67`; 4) Violated reliability (`8s hangs`), onboarding simplicity (`19.3`), and retention cohorts (`19.7`); 5) AI accelerates boilerplate while human devs enforce architecture, security audits (`1.0`), & automated `node:test` gates (`12.0`).
- **Worksheet Part C Key:** 1) `$12 * 0.70 - $1.40 = $7.00`; 2) `7000 / 7 = 1,000` subs; 3) LTV=`$7 * 10 = $70`; Ratio = `1.56 : 1` -> **NO** (`< 3:1`). Improve via qualifying for 15% Small Business App Store cut, annual pricing plans, & fixing onboarding to slash churn!
