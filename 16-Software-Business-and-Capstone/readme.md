# Chapter 16: Software Business, Agile Project Delivery & Semester Capstone (`Std 19.0`)

## Student Learning Objectives (Bloom's Taxonomy)
- **`[L1 Remember]`** Recall **Waterfall**, **Top-Down**, and **Agile/Scrum** workflows (`19.1`), App Store distribution policies (`19.2`), monetization models (`19.5`), and emerging AI/Edge trends (`19.8`).
- **`[L2 Understand]`** Contrast Agile iterative feedback against rigid linear Waterfall phases (`19.1`), and distinguish **MRR**, **CAC**, **LTV**, and **Churn** (`19.4–19.7`).
- **`[L3 Apply]`** Model software startup costs, App Store revenue splits (`15%–30%`), net profit margins, and break-even subscriber targets programmatically in JS (`19.4–19.5`).
- **`[L4 Analyze]`** Inspect failing product telemetry (high churn / high CAC / bloated MVP scope) to pinpoint business & engineering causes (`19.6–19.7`).
- **`[L5 Evaluate]`** Select deployment channels, pricing models, and MVP scope cuts to launch reliably within runway (`19.2–19.6`).

---

## 1. Software Methodologies, App Stores & Successful App Traits (`Std 19.1–19.3, 19.6`)
- **Methodology Comparison (`19.1`):**
  - **Waterfall:** Fixed linear phases (`Requirements -> Design -> Build -> Test -> Deploy`). Suits regulated hardware/compliance specs where changes are rare.
  - **Top-Down Design:** Breaks master system cleanly into modular sub-components down to atomic helpers (`Std 10.0` synergy).
  - **Agile / Scrum:** Time-boxed `2-week` sprints, Kanban backlogs, daily standups, incremental MVPs, and retrospectives optimizing fast adaptation (`19.6`).
- **Deployment & Quality Traits (`19.2–19.3`):** Publish via Apple App Store / Google Play (`review guidelines, privacy labels`), Web CI/CD, or npm; lasting apps pair zero-friction onboarding (`< 60s Time-to-Value`), rock-solid crash-free uptime, and sharp problem focus!

---

## 2. Software Costing, Monetization & Post-Launch Telemetry (`Std 19.4–19.5, 19.7–19.8`)
- **Full Cost Budget (`19.4`):** Engineering labor, cloud compute/storage/bandwidth, payment fees (`~2.9% + $0.30`), App Store platform commission (`15% Small Business / 30% Standard`), marketing CAC, & customer support.
- **Monetization Models (`19.5`):** **SaaS Subscriptions** (recurring MRR/ARR), **Freemium** (free baseline + Pro upgrade), **Pay-Per-Use API** (usage credits), **One-Time Purchase**, and **Ad-Supported**.
- **Healthy Unit Economics & Feedback (`19.7`):** Track **LTV** (`Lifetime Value`), **CAC** (`Customer Acquisition Cost`), healthy **`LTV : CAC >= 3 : 1`**, NPS feedback, & cohort retention before scaling marketing spend!
- **Industry Horizon (`19.8`):** AI-assisted coding/agents (human verification still mandatory!), Edge compute Workers, Zero-Trust security defaults, & Accessibility-first design.
