# Chapter 16: Software Business & Capstone
**AZ CTE Standard 19.0 (`19.1`–`19.8`)**

---

### `[L1 Remember -> L2 Understand]` Methodologies (`Std 19.1, 19.6`)

- **Waterfall:** Linear phases; fixed requirements up front.
- **Top-Down:** Decompose giant problems modularly downward.
- **Agile / Scrum:** `2-week` sprints, Kanban board, ship **MVP** early & inspect customer feedback!

---

### `[L3 Apply -> L4 Analyze]` App Cost & SaaS Economics (`Std 19.4–19.5, 19.7`)

- **MRR:** Monthly Recurring Revenue (`Subscribers × Monthly Price`)
- **Net Store Payout:** Gross minus App Store cut (`15%–30%`) & card fee
- **Break-Even Users:** `Fixed Monthly Cost / Net Profit Per User`
- **Golden Ratio (`19.7`):** Healthy **`LTV / CAC >= 3.0`**!

---

### `[L5 Evaluate -> L6 Create]` Launch Channels & Trends (`Std 19.2–19.3, 19.8`)

- **Stores & CI/CD (`19.2`):** App Store / Play Store reviews, SemVer releases, automated tests before push.
- **Winning App Traits (`19.3`):** Fast value, crash-free reliability, crisp UX/a11y.
- **Trends (`19.8`):** AI dev workflows, Edge Serverless, Zero-Trust Security!
