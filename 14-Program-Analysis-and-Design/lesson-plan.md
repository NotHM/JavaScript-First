# Lesson Plan 14.1: Program Analysis, SDLC & System Design (`Std 10.0`)

**Unit:** Unit 14 | **Duration:** 5 Class Periods × 50 min | **AZ CTE Standard:** `10.0` (`10.1`–`10.10`)

## Bloom's Objective Ladder
- **L1–L2:** Identify SDLC phases, RAD/RUP, HIPAA/PCI/GLBA, and contrast JS/Python/Java/C# and SQL/NoSQL.
- **L3 Apply:** Author Given/When/Then acceptance tests, problem decomposition, and telemetry emitters.
- **L4 Analyze:** Apply structured debugging steps (`reproduce -> isolate -> bisect -> verify`) and catch compliance misses.
- **L5 Evaluate / L6 Create:** Defend architecture stacks and draft full-system design specs & flowcharts.
