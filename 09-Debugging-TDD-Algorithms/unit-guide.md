# Unit 9 Guide: Testing, Debugging, Algorithms & ML Fairness Audits

**Course:** JavaScript First — High School CTE (Grades 11–12) & Community College CIS (AZ CTE CIP **11.0202.00**)  
**Semester Schedule:** Week 9 (Days 40–48 | 9 Class Periods × 50 Minutes incl. AI ML & Fairness Track)

## 1. Unit Overview
Unit 9 delivers dedicated mastery of **AZ CTE Standard 12.0 (Testing & Debugging Program Operation)**, **AZ CTE Standard 6.4–6.7 (Sorting & Searching Efficiency)**, and **CTE AI (`11.0102.00`) / Azure AI-900 (`AI Stds 1.0, 2.0, 6.0, 7.0`)**. Students classify syntax/semantic/logic/runtime failures, execute Red-Green-Refactor TDD with `node:test`, inspect call stacks via breakpoints, benchmark Linear vs. Binary Search and Bubble/Insertion/Merge Sort, and apply TDD rigor to **ML algorithms (OLS Regression, Logistic Thresholding, k-Means)**, **evaluation metrics (`RMSE`, $R^2$, Confusion Matrix `F1`, Bias/Variance)**, and **EEOC `4/5ths` (`80%`) Disparate Impact fairness auditing**.
