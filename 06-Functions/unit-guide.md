# Unit 6 Guide: Conditionals, Boolean Logic & Functions

**Course:** JavaScript First — Software & App Design (AZ CTE CIP **11.0202.00**)  
**Semester Schedule:** Week 6 (Days 25–29 | 5 Class Periods × 50 Minutes)

---

## 1. Unit Overview
Unit 6 merges **AZ CTE Standard 5.0 (Conditional Structures & Boolean Algebra)** with **modular Functions (`Std 5.5` & `Std 11.10–11.12`)**. Students construct complete Boolean truth tables (`AND`, `OR`, `NOT`, `NOR`, `XOR`), diagram decision trees, refactor nested conditionals into clean early-return guard clauses, build private state with closures, curry functional pipelines, and understand seeded pseudo-random number generators (PRNGs).
