## 1. Unit Overview

Unit 12 builds rigorous quantitative reasoning for software and autonomous systems engineers (**AZ CTE Standard 4.0** & **West-MEC Drone Technology `DT101` Stds `1.0–12.0, 23.0`**). In **Lesson 12.1**, students connect hardware capacity and clock speed units (`bits` to `Petabytes`, `kHz` to `GHz`), binary/hexadecimal number systems, GEMA precedence, engineering formulas, IEEE-754 floating-point precision hazards, and bitwise permission flags in Node.js. In **Lesson 12.2**, students apply GEMA, integer millivolts (`mV`), `Float64`, and trigonometry to **Props-Off Bench Safety**, **Ohm's Law ESC/motor current budgets**, **`3S–6S` LiPo battery chemistry & `80%` DoD hover runtimes**, **Four Forces & CW/CCW prop torque**, **Brushless `KV * V` & `>= 2:1` Thrust-to-Weight**, **Banked-Turn Load Factor ($1/\cos\theta$) & Density Altitude**, **WGS-84 Haversine GPS distance**, and **Photogrammetry GSD**.
