# Unit 5 Guide: Iterative Structures, Arrays, Matrices & AI Data Pipelines

**Course:** JavaScript First — Software & App Design (AZ CTE CIP **11.0202.00**)  
**Semester Schedule:** Weeks 4–5 (Days 18–27 | 10 Class Periods × 50 Minutes incl. AI Data Pipeline Track)

---

## 1. Unit Overview
Unit 5 delivers comprehensive coverage of **AZ CTE Standard 7.0 (Iterative Structures)**, **AZ CTE Standard 6.0 (Data Structures & Collections)**, and **West-MEC `AI101` Units 3–4 / `AI201` Unit 1 (`AI Stds 4.0, 5.0`)**. Students master loop mechanics (`while`, `for`, `for...of`, `.forEach()`, `break`/`continue`, nested loops, trace tables, and recursion) alongside core structures (1D Arrays, Hash Maps, 2D Matrices, Trees, Graphs, `JSON`, non-mutating `.map`/`.filter`/`.reduce`) and extend directly into **AI tabular data cleaning**, **missing-value imputation**, **PII anonymization**, **IQR/Z-score outlier detection**, **Min-Max vs. Z-Score scaling**, **One-Hot encoding**, **vector/matrix algebra**, and **EDA descriptive/correlation statistics**.
